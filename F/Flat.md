# Theme: Flat

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#002240`
- Foreground: `#2cc55d`
- Cursor: `#e5be0c`
- Cursor text: `#ffffff`
- Selection: `#792b9c`
- Selection text: `#ffffff`
- Contrast: 7.10
- Same brights: 0
- Licence: unstated
- Licence from:
- Default: no
- Credit: The Flat theme was created by [ahmetsulek](https://github.com/ahmetsulek/flat-terminal)
- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/Flat](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/Flat)

The dark terminal theme Flat: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
