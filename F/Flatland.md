# Theme: Flatland

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#1d1f21`
- Foreground: `#b8dbef`
- Cursor: `#708284`
- Cursor text: `#002831`
- Selection: `#2b2a24`
- Selection text: `#ffffff`
- Contrast: 11.35
- Same brights: 3
- Licence: unstated
- Licence from:
- Default: no
- Featured: no

The dark terminal theme Flatland: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
