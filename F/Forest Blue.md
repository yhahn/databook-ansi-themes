- Dark: yes
- Background: `#051519`
- Foreground: `#e2d8cd`
- Cursor: `#9e9ecb`
- Cursor text: `#000000`
- Selection: `#4d4d4d`
- Selection text: `#ffffff`
- Contrast: 13.25
- Same brights: 1
- Licence: unstated
- Licence from:
- Default: no

The dark terminal theme Forest Blue: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
