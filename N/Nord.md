- Background: `#2e3440`
- Foreground: `#d8dee9`
- Cursor: `#eceff4`
- Cursor text: `#282828`
- Selection: `#eceff4`

The dark terminal theme Nord: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
