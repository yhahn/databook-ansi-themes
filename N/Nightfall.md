# Theme: Nightfall

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#1a140e`
- Foreground: `#f6e8d0`
- Cursor: `#d4a85c`
- Cursor text: `#1a140e`
- Selection: `#3b2a1a`
- Selection text: `#f6e8d0`
- Contrast: 15.10
- Same brights: 0
- Licence: unstated

- Default: no

- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/Nightfall](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/Nightfall)

The dark terminal theme Nightfall: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
