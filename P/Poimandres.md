# Theme: Poimandres

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#1a1e28`
- Foreground: `#a6accd`
- Cursor: `#ffffff`
- Cursor text: `#1a1e29`
- Selection: `#a6accd`
- Selection text: `#ffffff`
- Contrast: 7.46
- Same brights: 4
- Licence: unstated

- Default: no
- Featured: no

The dark terminal theme Poimandres: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
