- Dark: no
- Background: `#fdf7e7`
- Foreground: `#1a1a1a`
- Cursor: `#7a5700`
- Cursor text: `#fffaf0`
- Selection: `#e5dcc3`
- Selection text: `#1a1a1a`
- Contrast: 16.27
- Same brights: 0
- Licence: unstated

The light terminal theme Parchment: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
