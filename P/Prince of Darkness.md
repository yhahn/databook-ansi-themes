- Cursor: `#b47dff`
- Cursor text: `#0a0714`

The dark terminal theme Prince of Darkness: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
