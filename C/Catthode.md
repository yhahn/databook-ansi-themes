# Theme: Catthode

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#000000`
- Foreground: `#ffffff`
- Cursor: `#ff9e3b`
- Cursor text: `#000000`
- Selection: `#2d2d2d`
- Selection text: `#ffffff`
- Contrast: 21.00
- Same brights: 5
- Licence: unstated
- Licence from:
- Default: no
- Credit: The Catthode theme was created by the [Catthode](https://github.com/catthode) project.
- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/Catthode](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/Catthode)

The dark terminal theme Catthode: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
