- Dark: yes
- Background: `#40318d`
- Foreground: `#7869c4`
- Cursor: `#7869c4`
- Cursor text: `#40318d`
- Selection: `#7869c4`
- Selection text: `#40318d`
- Contrast: 2.26
- Same brights: 6
- Licence: unstated

The dark terminal theme C64: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
