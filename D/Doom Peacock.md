# Theme: Doom Peacock

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#2b2a27`
- Foreground: `#ede0ce`
- Cursor: `#9c9c9d`
- Cursor text: `#36312b`
- Selection: `#a60033`
- Selection text: `#ffffff`
- Contrast: 11.04
- Same brights: 0
- Licence: unstated
- Licence from:
- Default: no
- Credit: The Doom Peacock theme was based on [Emacs Doom Peacock theme](https://github.com/hlissner/emacs-doom-themes)
- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/Doom Peacock](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/Doom%20Peacock)

The dark terminal theme Doom Peacock: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
