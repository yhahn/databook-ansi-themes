- Background: `#0f1318`
- Foreground: `#dbe0e8`
- Cursor: `#fa94cd`
- Cursor text: `#0f1318`
- Selection: `#2b2f35`
- Selection text: `#dbe0e8`
- Contrast: 14.06
- Same brights: 1
- Licence: unstated

- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/datum (dark)](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/datum%20(dark))

The dark terminal theme datum (dark): its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
