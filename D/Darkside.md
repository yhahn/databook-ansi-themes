- Dark: yes
- Background: `#222324`
- Foreground: `#bababa`
- Cursor: `#bbbbbb`
- Cursor text: `#ffffff`
- Selection: `#303333`
- Selection text: `#bababa`
- Contrast: 8.11
- Same brights: 1
- Licence: unstated

The dark terminal theme Darkside: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
