- Dark: yes
- Background: `#0b1220`
- Foreground: `#e6edf3`
- Cursor: `#8ecbff`
- Cursor text: `#0b1220`
- Selection: `#284361`
- Selection text: `#f5f8fc`
- Contrast: 15.85
- Same brights: 0
- Licence: unstated

The dark terminal theme Eventide: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
