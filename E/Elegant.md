# Theme: Elegant

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#292b31`
- Foreground: `#ced2d6`
- Cursor: `#00beff`
- Cursor text: `#ffffff`
- Selection: `#d5d5d5`
- Selection text: `#144385`
- Contrast: 9.31
- Same brights: 5
- Licence: unstated
- Licence from:
- Default: no
- Credit: The Elegant theme was created by [lukgri](https://github.com/lukgri)
- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/Elegant](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/Elegant)

The dark terminal theme Elegant: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
