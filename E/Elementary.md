- Background: `#181818`
- Foreground: `#efefef`
- Cursor: `#bbbbbb`
- Cursor text: `#ffffff`
- Selection: `#b5d5ff`

The dark terminal theme Elementary: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
