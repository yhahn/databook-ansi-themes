# Theme: Mellifluous

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#1a1a1a`
- Foreground: `#dadada`
- Cursor: `#bfad9e`
- Cursor text: `#1a1a1a`
- Selection: `#2d2d2d`
- Selection text: `#c0af8c`
- Contrast: 12.45
- Same brights: 0
- Licence: unstated
- Licence from:
- Default: no
- Featured: no
- Credit: The Mellifluous theme was adapted by [rektdeckard](https://github.com/rektdeckard) from the NeoVim scheme of the same name by [ramojus](https://github.com/ramojus/mellifluous.nvim)
- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/Mellifluous](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/Mellifluous)

The dark terminal theme Mellifluous: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
