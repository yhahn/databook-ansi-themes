# Theme: Miasma

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#222222`
- Foreground: `#c2c2b0`
- Cursor: `#c7c7c7`
- Cursor text: `#959595`
- Selection: `#e5c47b`
- Selection text: `#000000`
- Contrast: 8.82
- Same brights: 7
- Licence: unstated
- Licence from:
- Default: no
- Credit: The Miasma theme was created by [xero](https://github.com/xero).
- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/Miasma](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/Miasma)

The dark terminal theme Miasma: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
