- Foreground: `#bbbbbb`
- Cursor: `#bbbbbb`
- Cursor text: `#ffffff`
- Selection: `#555555`
- Selection text: `#f2f2f2`
- Contrast: 10.94
- Same brights: 0
- Licence: unstated

The dark terminal theme Mathias: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
