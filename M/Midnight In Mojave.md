# Theme: Midnight In Mojave

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#1e1e1e`
- Foreground: `#ffffff`
- Cursor: `#32d74b`
- Cursor text: `#1c1c1c`
- Selection: `#4a504d`
- Selection text: `#ffffff`
- Contrast: 16.67
- Same brights: 7
- Licence: unstated

The dark terminal theme Midnight In Mojave: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
