# Theme: Andromeda

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#262a33`
- Foreground: `#e5e5e5`
- Cursor: `#f8f8f0`
- Cursor text: `#b5b5a8`
- Selection: `#5a5c62`
- Selection text: `#ece7e7`
- Contrast: 11.41
- Same brights: 7
- Licence: unstated

- Default: no

- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/Andromeda](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/Andromeda)

The dark terminal theme Andromeda: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
