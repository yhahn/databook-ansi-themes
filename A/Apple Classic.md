- Dark: yes
- Background: `#2c2b2b`
- Foreground: `#d5a200`
- Cursor: `#c7c7c7`
- Cursor text: `#8c8c8c`
- Selection: `#6b5b02`
- Selection text: `#67e000`
- Contrast: 6.05
- Same brights: 0
- Licence: unstated

The dark terminal theme Apple Classic: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
