# Theme: Adwaita Dark

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#1c1c1f`
- Foreground: `#ffffff`
- Cursor: `#ffffff`
- Cursor text: `#1c1c1f`
- Selection: `#ffffff`
- Selection text: `#1c1c1f`
- Contrast: 17.00
- Same brights: 0
- Licence: unstated
- Licence from:
- Default: no
- Credit: The themes Adwaita and Adwaita Dark are based on the GNOME desktop environment's default Adwaita palette and were adapted for iTerm2 by [cnavajas](https://github.com/cnavajas), with inspiration from vixalien's palette.
- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/Adwaita Dark](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/Adwaita%20Dark)

The dark terminal theme Adwaita Dark: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
