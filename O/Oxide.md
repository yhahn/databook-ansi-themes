# Theme: Oxide

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#161616`
- Foreground: `#cecece`
- Cursor: `#cecece`
- Cursor text: `#161616`
- Selection: `#262626`
- Selection text: `#cecece`
- Contrast: 11.50
- Same brights: 0
- Licence: unstated
- Licence from:
- Default: no

- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/Oxide](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/Oxide)

The dark terminal theme Oxide: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
