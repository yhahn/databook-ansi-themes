- Background: `#f7f8fa`
- Foreground: `#2e3440`
- Cursor: `#3879c5`
- Cursor text: `#f7f8fa`

- Same brights: 0
- Licence: unstated

The light terminal theme Onenord Light: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
