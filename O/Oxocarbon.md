# Theme: Oxocarbon

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#161616`
- Foreground: `#f2f4f8`
- Cursor: `#ffffff`
- Cursor text: `#000000`
- Selection: `#393939`
- Selection text: `#626262`
- Contrast: 16.43
- Same brights: 7
- Licence: unstated
- Licence from:
- Default: no
- Credit: The Oxocarbon theme was created by [Nyoom Engineering](https://github.com/nyoom-engineering) and ported to iTerm2 by [Hanna Rose](https://github.com/hqnna).
- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/Oxocarbon](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/Oxocarbon)

The dark terminal theme Oxocarbon: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
