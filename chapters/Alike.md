# Chapter: Alike

- Ask: Which themes are alike?

Two themes are alike here where their eighteen colours (background, text and the sixteen) lie, on the average, under 0.01 apart in OKLab: half the step an eye is commonly held to see (0.02). Copies are alike in every colour. Cursor and selection are left out: a terminal's text is drawn in the eighteen.

## Copies
