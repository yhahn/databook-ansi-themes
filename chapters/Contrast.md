# Chapter: Contrast

- Ask: Which themes read best?

Contrast is a theme's text on its own background, WCAG's ratio: 1 where they are the same, 21 for black on white; 4.5 reads well (AA), 7 very well (AAA). Every theme's is on its own page and its letter's.

## How many read well

**How many themes read well?** 791 of the 816 read well (4.5 or more) and 724 very well (7 or more); 25 fall short. The median theme's contrast is 12.09.

The themes by the contrast of their text, 4.5 marked.
