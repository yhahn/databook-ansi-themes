# Chapter: Dark or light

- Ask: How many themes are dark, and how dark?

A theme is dark where white text reads better on its background than black (WCAG's contrast). Lightness here is OKLab's, 0% black and 100% white, an even step of it an even step to the eye. Every theme is on its letter's page too, by name; [the front page](../README.md) asks the other questions.

## How dark

**How many themes are dark, and how dark?** 634 of the 816 are dark, 78%. The median dark background is 22% light; the median light one 97%.

The themes by their background's lightness, a tenth a bar.
