# Theme: Rouge 2

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#17182b`
- Foreground: `#a2a3aa`
- Cursor: `#969e92`
- Cursor text: `#ffffff`
- Selection: `#5d5d6b`
- Selection text: `#dfe5ee`
- Contrast: 6.95
- Same brights: 2
- Licence: unstated
- Licence from:
- Default: no
- Credit: The Rouge 2 theme was created by [Josef Aidt](https://github.com/josefaidt/rouge-theme) and ported to iTerm2 by [Sam Rose](https://github.com/samrose3).
- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/Rouge 2](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/Rouge%202)

The dark terminal theme Rouge 2: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
