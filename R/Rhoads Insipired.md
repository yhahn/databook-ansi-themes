- Background: `#0a0a0a`
- Foreground: `#f5f5f0`
- Cursor: `#a855f7`
- Cursor text: `#f5f5f0`
- Selection: `#f4b942`

- Same brights: 0

The dark terminal theme Rhoads Insipired: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
