- Background: `#292a44`
- Foreground: `#e8e6ed`
- Cursor: `#b89bf9`
- Cursor text: `#292a44`
- Selection: `#663399`

- Contrast: 11.25
- Same brights: 0
- Licence: unstated

- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/Rebecca](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/Rebecca)

The dark terminal theme Rebecca: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
