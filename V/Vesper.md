# Theme: Vesper

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#101010`
- Foreground: `#ffffff`
- Cursor: `#acb1ab`
- Cursor text: `#ffffff`
- Selection: `#988049`
- Selection text: `#b9beb8`
- Contrast: 19.03
- Same brights: 0
- Licence: unstated
- Licence from:
- Default: no
- Credit: The Vesper theme was originally created by [raunofreiberg](https://github.com/raunofreiberg) for VSCode, ported to iTerm2 by [geoffreybrunet](https://github.com/GeoffreyBrunet) and added by [ubmit](https://github.com/ubmit)
- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/Vesper](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/Vesper)

The dark terminal theme Vesper: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
