- Background: `#040404`
- Foreground: `#feffff`
- Cursor: `#e0d000`
- Cursor text: `#000000`
- Selection: `#606060`
- Selection text: `#ffffff`
- Contrast: 20.47
- Same brights: 0

The dark terminal theme 12-bit Rainbow: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
