# Theme: Lab Fox

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#2e2e2e`
- Foreground: `#ffffff`
- Cursor: `#7f7f7f`
- Cursor text: `#b2b2b2`
- Selection: `#cb392e`
- Selection text: `#ffffff`
- Contrast: 13.58
- Same brights: 1
- Licence: unstated
- Licence from:
- Default: no
- Credit: The Lab Fox theme was created by [theatlasroom](https://github.com/theatlasroom) inspired by the [Gitlab design system](https://design.gitlab.com)
- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/Lab Fox](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/Lab%20Fox)

The dark terminal theme Lab Fox: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
