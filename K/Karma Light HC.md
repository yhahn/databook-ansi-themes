# Theme: Karma Light HC

- Dark: no
- Background: `#ffffff`
- Foreground: `#000000`
- Cursor: `#4d2d9a`
- Cursor text: `#ffffff`
- Selection: `#cfcad8`
- Selection text: `#000000`
- Contrast: 21.00
- Same brights: 6

The light terminal theme Karma Light HC: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
