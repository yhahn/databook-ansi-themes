- Background: `#000000`
- Foreground: `#ffffff`

The dark terminal theme Karma Dark HC: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
