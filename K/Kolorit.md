# Theme: Kolorit

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#1d1a1e`
- Foreground: `#efecec`
- Cursor: `#c7c7c7`
- Cursor text: `#8c8c8c`
- Selection: `#e1925c`
- Selection text: `#1d1a1e`
- Contrast: 14.67
- Same brights: 7
- Licence: unstated
- Licence from:
- Default: no
- Credit: The Kolorit theme was created by [thomaspaulmann](https://github.com/thomaspaulmann)
- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/Kolorit](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/Kolorit)

The dark terminal theme Kolorit: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
