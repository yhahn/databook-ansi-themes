- Dark: yes
- Background: `#14181f`
- Foreground: `#e2def0`
- Cursor: `#ebd870`
- Cursor text: `#14181f`
- Selection: `#2f2d3d`
- Selection text: `#e2def0`
- Contrast: 13.51
- Same brights: 1
- Licence: unstated

The dark terminal theme Karma Dark Dimmed: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
