# Theme: Karma Dark

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#0a0e14`
- Foreground: `#f7f1ff`
- Cursor: `#fce566`
- Cursor text: `#0a0e14`
- Selection: `#3a384a`
- Selection text: `#f7f1ff`
- Contrast: 17.48
- Same brights: 1
- Licence: unstated
- Licence from:
- Default: no

- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/Karma Dark](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/Karma%20Dark)

The dark terminal theme Karma Dark: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
