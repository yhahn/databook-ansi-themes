# Theme: Karma Light

- Dark: no
- Background: `#ffffff`
- Foreground: `#0a0e14`
- Cursor: `#a86efd`
- Cursor text: `#ffffff`
- Selection: `#e5e3ea`
- Selection text: `#0a0e14`
- Contrast: 19.34
- Same brights: 4
- Licence: unstated

The light terminal theme Karma Light: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
