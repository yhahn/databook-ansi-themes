# Theme: Tomorrow

- Dark: no
- Background: `#ffffff`
- Foreground: `#4d4d4c`
- Cursor: `#4d4d4c`
- Cursor text: `#ffffff`
- Selection: `#d6d6d6`
- Selection text: `#4d4d4c`
- Contrast: 8.46
- Same brights: 7

The light terminal theme Tomorrow: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
