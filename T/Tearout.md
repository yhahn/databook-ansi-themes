# Theme: Tearout

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#34392d`
- Foreground: `#f4d2ae`
- Cursor: `#d7c483`
- Cursor text: `#141415`
- Selection: `#e4c47a`
- Selection text: `#000000`
- Contrast: 8.30
- Same brights: 7
- Licence: unstated
- Licence from:
- Default: no
- Credit: The Tearout theme was created by [leobeosab](https://github.com/leobeosab)
- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/Tearout](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/Tearout)

The dark terminal theme Tearout: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
