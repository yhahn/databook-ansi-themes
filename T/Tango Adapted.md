- Foreground: `#000000`

- Selection: `#c1deff`

- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/Tango Adapted](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/Tango%20Adapted)

The light terminal theme Tango Adapted: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
