- Dark: yes
- Background: `#1d1f21`
- Foreground: `#c5c8c6`
- Cursor: `#c5c8c6`
- Cursor text: `#1d1f21`
- Selection: `#373b41`

The dark terminal theme Tomorrow Night: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
