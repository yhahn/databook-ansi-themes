- Dark: yes
- Background: `#262626`
- Foreground: `#e5e5e5`
- Cursor: `#e5e5e5`
- Cursor text: `#262626`
- Selection: `#475569`

- Contrast: 12.01
- Same brights: 0
- Licence: unstated

The dark terminal theme Tailwind Dark: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
