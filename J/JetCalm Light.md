- Background: `#ededed`
- Foreground: `#1c2410`
- Cursor: `#082108`
- Cursor text: `#ededed`
- Selection: `#3c444d`

- Same brights: 0
- Licence: unstated

- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/JetCalm Light](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/JetCalm%20Light)

The light terminal theme JetCalm Light: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
