# Theme: Breeze

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#31363b`
- Foreground: `#eff0f1`
- Cursor: `#eff0f1`
- Cursor text: `#31363b`
- Selection: `#eff0f1`
- Selection text: `#31363b`
- Contrast: 10.69
- Same brights: 0
- Licence: unstated
- Licence from:
- Default: no
- Credit: The Breeze theme was based on [the Breeze color scheme](https://github.com/KDE/konsole/blob/master/data/color-schemes/Breeze.colorscheme) by [KDE](https://kde.org) and ported to iTerm by [nieltg](https://github.com/nieltg)
- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/Breeze](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/Breeze)

The dark terminal theme Breeze: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
