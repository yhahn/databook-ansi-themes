# Theme: Borland

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#0000a4`
- Foreground: `#ffff4e`
- Cursor: `#ffa560`
- Cursor text: `#ffffff`
- Selection: `#a4a4a4`
- Selection text: `#0000a4`
- Contrast: 12.80
- Same brights: 0
- Licence: unstated
- Licence from:
- Default: no

The dark terminal theme Borland: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
