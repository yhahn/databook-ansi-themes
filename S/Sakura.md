- Background: `#18131e`
- Foreground: `#dd7bdc`

- Cursor text: `#24242e`
- Selection: `#c05cbf`

- Same brights: 0
- Licence: unstated

The dark terminal theme Sakura: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
