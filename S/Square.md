- Background: `#1a1a1a`
- Foreground: `#acacab`
- Cursor: `#fcfbcc`
- Cursor text: `#000000`
- Selection: `#4d4d4d`

- Same brights: 0

The dark terminal theme Square: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
