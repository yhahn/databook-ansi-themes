# Theme: Smyck

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#1b1b1b`
- Foreground: `#f7f7f7`
- Cursor: `#bbbbbb`
- Cursor text: `#ffffff`
- Selection: `#207483`
- Selection text: `#f7f7f7`
- Contrast: 16.08
- Same brights: 0
- Licence: unstated
- Licence from:
- Default: no
- Credit: The Smyck theme was found [here](https://github.com/hukl/Smyck-Color-Scheme)
- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/Smyck](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/Smyck)

The dark terminal theme Smyck: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
