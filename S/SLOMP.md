# Theme: SLOMP

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#1e1e1e`
- Foreground: `#d4d4d4`
- Cursor: `#c86ead`
- Cursor text: `#1e1e1e`
- Selection: `#0c363f`
- Selection text: `#d4d4d4`
- Contrast: 11.25
- Same brights: 4
- Licence: unstated

- Default: no
- Featured: no

The dark terminal theme SLOMP: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
