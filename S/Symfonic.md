# Theme: Symfonic

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#000000`
- Foreground: `#ffffff`
- Cursor: `#dc322f`
- Cursor text: `#ffffff`
- Selection: `#073642`
- Selection text: `#ffffff`
- Contrast: 21.00
- Same brights: 7
- Licence: unstated
- Licence from:
- Default: no
- Credit: The scheme Symfonic was inspired by the color scheme used in the [documentation](http://symfony.com/doc/current/book/index.html) for the [Symfony](http://www.symfony.com) PHP Framework.
- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/Symfonic](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/Symfonic)

The dark terminal theme Symfonic: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
