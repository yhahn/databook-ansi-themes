- Background: `#1e1d40`

- Cursor text: `#98998e`

The dark terminal theme Shades Of Purple: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
