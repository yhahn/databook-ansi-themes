# Theme: Snazzy

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#1e1f29`
- Foreground: `#ebece6`
- Cursor: `#e4e4e4`
- Cursor text: `#a9a9a9`
- Selection: `#81aec6`
- Selection text: `#000000`
- Contrast: 13.77
- Same brights: 7
- Licence: unstated
- Licence from:
- Default: no
- Credit: The Snazzy Soft theme was created by [liby](https://github.com/liby) inspired by [Sindre Sorhus](https://github.com/sindresorhus)'s [iterm2-snazzy](https://github.com/sindresorhus/iterm2-snazzy), offering a gentler color palette
- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/Snazzy](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/Snazzy)

The dark terminal theme Snazzy: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
