# Theme: Subliminal

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#282c35`
- Foreground: `#d4d4d4`
- Cursor: `#c7c7c7`
- Cursor text: `#8c8c8c`
- Selection: `#484e5b`
- Selection text: `#ffffff`
- Contrast: 9.43
- Same brights: 8
- Licence: unstated
- Licence from:
- Default: no

- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/Subliminal](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/Subliminal)

The dark terminal theme Subliminal: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
