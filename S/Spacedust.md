# Theme: Spacedust

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#0a1e24`
- Foreground: `#ecf0c1`
- Cursor: `#708284`
- Cursor text: `#002831`
- Selection: `#0a385c`
- Selection text: `#ffffff`
- Contrast: 14.54
- Same brights: 0
- Licence: unstated
- Licence from:
- Default: no
- Featured: no
- Credit: The Spacedust theme was created by [mhallendal](https://github.com/mhallendal/spacedust-theme) and ported to iTerm by [Couto](https://github.com/Couto)
- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/Spacedust](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/Spacedust)

The dark terminal theme Spacedust: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
