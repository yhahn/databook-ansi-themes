- Dark: yes
- Background: `#1d2021`
- Foreground: `#d4be98`
- Cursor: `#ffffff`
- Cursor text: `#000000`
- Selection: `#2b2c3f`
- Selection text: `#7cfb70`
- Contrast: 9.07
- Same brights: 4

The dark terminal theme Gruvbox Material: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
