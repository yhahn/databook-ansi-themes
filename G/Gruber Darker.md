# Theme: Gruber Darker

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#181818`
- Foreground: `#e4e4e4`
- Cursor: `#ffdb00`
- Cursor text: `#a6a6a6`
- Selection: `#ffffff`
- Selection text: `#54494e`
- Contrast: 13.97
- Same brights: 4
- Licence: unstated
- Licence from:
- Default: no
- Featured: no
- Credit: The Gruber Darker theme was inspired by [Alexey Kutepov](https://github.com/rexim)
- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/Gruber Darker](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/Gruber%20Darker)

The dark terminal theme Gruber Darker: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
