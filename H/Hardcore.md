- Dark: yes
- Background: `#121212`
- Foreground: `#a0a0a0`
- Cursor: `#bbbbbb`
- Cursor text: `#ffffff`
- Selection: `#453b39`

- Contrast: 7.16
- Same brights: 2
- Licence: unstated

The dark terminal theme Hardcore: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
