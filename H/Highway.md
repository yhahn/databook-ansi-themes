# Theme: Highway

- Dark: yes
- Background: `#222225`
- Foreground: `#ededed`
- Cursor: `#e0d9b9`
- Cursor text: `#1f192a`
- Selection: `#384564`
- Selection text: `#ededed`
- Contrast: 13.55
- Same brights: 0
- Licence: unstated
- Licence from:
- Default: no

- Made from: [iTerm2-Color-Schemes, ghostty/Highway](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/c379d7a31af861838a017468b03120c8970eaa00/ghostty/Highway)

The dark terminal theme Highway: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
