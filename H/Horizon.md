- Background: `#1c1e26`
- Foreground: `#d5d8da`
- Cursor: `#6c6f93`

The dark terminal theme Horizon: its background, text, cursor and selection, and the sixteen colours a terminal names 0 to 15. Contrast is its text's against its background (WCAG; 4.5 reads well, 7 very well).
